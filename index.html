01What Idira actually does

Idira consolidates what used to be separate identity tools — vaulting, session brokering, discovery, governance, and secrets management — into one platform built around three jobs.

  1. Discover identity risk

    Continuously surface every identity, entitlement, and access path across the enterprise — human accounts, service accounts, API keys, and autonomous agents alike — so nothing is managed by guesswork.

  2. Control every privilege

    Replace static, always-on access with zero standing privilege and just-in-time enforcement, so elevated rights exist only for the moment they're needed and are revoked automatically after.

  3. Automate governance

    Turn compliance from a periodic scramble into a continuous process by applying AI-assisted policy across the full identity lifecycle — onboarding, access reviews, and offboarding included.
