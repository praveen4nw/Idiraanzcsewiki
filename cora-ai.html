Overview / 06 Built-in Assistant

CORA AI

CyberArk's assistant, built into every corner of the Idira platform — talk to it the way you'd talk to a colleague, and it either answers or acts.

01Two things it does

Answers questions

Ask about specifics in your own environment — who has access to a given Safe, what's configured on a particular platform — and CORA AI answers using official Idira knowledge sources, now including CyberArk's Knowledge Base articles alongside product documentation.

Performs tasks

Beyond answering, it can act: managing accounts and Safes, approving access requests, and retrieving platform information, in natural language rather than a specific command syntax.

02Where it shows up

CORA AI is woven into ISPSS services and engines rather than bolted on as a separate tool — it's available throughout the platform, across every space and service a given user is authorized to reach. Access is role-based: a user needs at least one qualifying administrative or auditor role before CORA AI becomes available to them for a given service.

03Session review, automated

One of the more concrete time-savers: instead of a human scrubbing through hours of recorded privileged sessions, CORA AI detects and highlights the most critical information on its own — and, per the ISPSS updates, now produces textual summaries and audits of SSH and RDP sessions specifically.

04Staying in control of it

SETUP → AI PREFERENCES AI features and tools can be disabled or enabled individually from the Setup space, so teams can opt out of specific CORA AI capabilities without losing the rest of the platform's functionality.
ROLE-GATED ACCESS Because availability is tied to administrative or auditor roles, CORA AI's reach is scoped by the same access model that governs everything else on the platform — it doesn't get a separate, broader permission set.
Related CORA AI is the interface layer on top of everything else on this site — it's how you'd typically interact with risk scores, ITDR alerts, and discovery results day to day, rather than a separate capability on its own.
