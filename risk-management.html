Overview / 02 Risk Management

Reducing identity risk continuously

Idira treats identity risk as something to be measured and driven down constantly, not something to be reported on once a quarter.

109 : 1 Ratio of machine and AI identities to human identities in a typical enterprise today.

01Why risk keeps growing

Elevated access used to be reserved for a small group of administrators. AI adoption has changed that: every application, pipeline, and now every autonomous agent can operate with meaningful privilege, and each one is a new path into sensitive systems. Idira's argument is that identity itself has become the effective perimeter, so risk management has to work at the level of identities and entitlements, not just networks.

02How Idira approaches it

AContinuous discovery

Every identity and entitlement — human, machine, agentic — and every access path between them is kept current automatically, rather than relying on periodic manual audits.

BAI-driven risk scoring

Billions of access events are distilled into prioritized risk signals, so security teams can act on the paths that matter instead of triaging noise.

CZero standing privilege

Access is granted just-in-time and revoked automatically at session close, replacing always-on admin rights that sit unused — and unwatched — most of the time.

03Secrets and machine identities

Risk management extends to non-human identities as well: unmanaged secrets, exposed API keys, and workload credentials are remediated and brought under modern secrets management, with governance and reporting across vaults so audit and compliance requirements are satisfied without a separate, manual process.
