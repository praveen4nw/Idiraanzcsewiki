Discovery SaaS

You can't secure what you don't know exists. Discovery is the step that builds an accurate inventory before any risk scoring or governance can mean anything.

01Why discovery is scoped the way it is

Idira's own services are already integrated and visible through the Identity Security Platform Shared Services layer, so they don't need a separate discovery step. Discovery SaaS exists specifically for everything outside Idira — the identity governance, MFA, identity provider, and PAM tools an organization has accumulated over time from other vendors.

02How it's typically done

STEP — CMDB check Start with the IT inventory system or configuration management database, checking for identity security tools such as IGA, MFA, identity providers, and PAM platforms — most CMDBs already track these by vendor, version, and system.
STEP — Peer input Where the asset inventory isn't reliable, discovery falls back to conversations with peers and management inside the IT security organization, who typically know which tools are actually in use even if the CMDB doesn't.
STEP — Onboarding Once discovered, non-Idira services and the accounts within them can be brought under the same web application integration and access controls applied to everything else on the platform.
